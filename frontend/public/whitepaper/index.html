1. Abstract

LibertyTorch is a community token built on Torch, deployed on Robinhood Chain. Its brand is Lady Liberty — the Statue of Liberty reimagined as a friendly character, torch raised high, mint green flame burning bright.

The core utility is a community raffle. The 3% buy tax funds an ETH prize vault. Holding $LIBERTY makes you eligible. Sending dust coins to the vault boosts your entries. Every hour, three winners share the vault.

There are no team tokens, no vesting schedules, no hidden allocations. The community owns the float from day one.

6. The Raffle — Hold to Enter

Hold $LIBERTY to be eligible. Send dust coins to boost your entries. Every hour, three winners split the vault.

How to boost your entries

Send any amount of any ERC-20 token to the LibertyTorch vault address. The USD value of your deposit is calculated via live oracle pricing — that USD value becomes your entry weight. The more dust you send, the higher your weight. Gas is paid by the project where possible — you should not need to spend ETH to enter.

Vault funding — two streams

Tax stream: 1.35% of every $LIBERTY buy automatically flows into the vault — on-chain, every trade.

Dust stream: deposited tokens are held in the vault, contributing to the pool. More dust = bigger vault = bigger prizes.

No. The 3% buy tax is set at launch and fixed forever.

How do I enter the raffle?

Any ERC-20 token. USD value is calculated via oracle pricing.

Entry weight = $LIBERTY holdings + USD value of dust deposits. Draw is random, weighted by total score.

ETH, sent directly to winning wallets. No conversion, no swap.

How often are draws?

Hourly. Three winners per draw: 50% / 30% / 20%.

Where do I get ETH for Robinhood Chain?
