Level 2 · Inside the copper

Green outside. Copper inside.

Small holdings, one shared prize vault, three steps up. The hourly draw is on-chain and automatic.

  1. 01

    Bring your dust

    Hold at least $10 in $LIBERTY for your base entry. Then link up to 5 secondary wallets holding crypto dust. Sign a message to prove control — no gas, no transaction.

  2. 02

    Earn entries

    Every $0.50 in dust from any supported token earns 1 entry. Each token gets its own live price feed. Maximum 5 entries per linked wallet per cycle. A 3% protocol fee comes off each deposit.

  3. 03

    Draw and share out

    Every hour, if the prize pool has at least $50 in ETH, 10 unique wallets win. Dust is automatically swapped to ETH and split into the pool. If the pool is below the threshold the drawing is skipped and funds roll over. Winners are excluded for 24 hours.

Level 5 · The Liberty Bell · 305 ft

When the Bell Tolls, Somebody Gets Paid.

The Liberty Bell receives 4% of every funded raffle into a growing jackpot. Every eligible hour, there is an independent 1-in-72 chance (~1.39%) that the Bell rings and one lucky holder takes the pot.

🔔

Independent Trigger

The Bell tests a fixed 1-in-72 probability at every eligible hourly check. The number of participants does not change the odds.

📈

Always Growing

4% of every funded raffle rolls into the jackpot. Even on quiet days it keeps building.

⚖️

Shared Cooldown

Bell winners share the same 24-hour cooldown as regular raffle winners. A recent regular winner cannot immediately win the jackpot, and vice versa.

🔗

On-Chain & Automatic

The trigger is verified on-chain. Lady Liberty does not promise what she cannot keep.

    Questions the pigeon asked.

    He asked them all. Loudly. On the windowsill.

    What counts as dust?

    Dust is any leftover ERC-20 token in your wallet that has a live price feed and is allow-listed on the vault. Instead of sitting there doing nothing, deposit it and earn raffle entries — every $0.50 of dust earns 1 entry, up to 5 per linked wallet.

    Which assets can go in?

    The vault accepts any allow-listed ERC-20 token with a live price feed. Accumulated dust is periodically converted to ETH: 50% goes to the prize pool and 50% goes back to the protocol. The current supported list is shown in the vault section.

    Why is the vault empty?

    The vault is live on-chain. Deposits open once supported dust tokens are configured and the $LIBERTY token launches. Check the ledger section to connect your wallet.

    How is my entry value worked out?

    The oracle values your deposit in USD when it lands. The 3% protocol fee comes off, and the net USD value is your entry weight. Each linked wallet earns up to 5 dust entries per hour (1 entry per $0.50). Dust tokens are converted to ETH — your entries are weighted, not ticket counts.

    What do winners get?

    ETH from the prize pool. Dust deposits are regularly swapped to ETH and split 50% to the prize pool and 50% back to the protocol treasury. Winners receive their payout share in ETH.

    What is the Liberty Bell jackpot?

    The Liberty Bell is a growing jackpot that receives 4% of every funded raffle. Every eligible hour, there is an independent 1-in-72 chance (~1.39%) that the bell rings and one lucky holder takes the pot. It is drawn automatically on-chain.
