1. Abstract

LibertyTorch is a community token built on Torch, deployed on Robinhood Chain. Its brand is Lady Liberty — the Statue of Liberty reimagined as a friendly character, torch raised high, mint green flame burning bright.

The core utility is an automated RWA raffle. Holding $LIBERTY earns base raffle entries. Linking secondary wallets and contributing RWA dust earns additional entries. Every hour, a funded drawing selects 10 unique winners who share the prize pool in kind.

The 3% buy tax funds the raffle. There are no team tokens, no vesting schedules, no hidden allocations. The community owns the float from day one.

6. The Raffle — Entry System

Who Can Enter

Hold at least $10 USD worth of $LIBERTY in your primary wallet for one base raffle entry. Link up to five verified secondary wallets to contribute RWA dust and earn additional entries. All entries are credited to your primary Liberty account.

Entry Calculation

Base Entry

Hold $10+ USD in $LIBERTY in your primary wallet → 1 base entry.

Dust Entries

RWA dust must be contributed through the LibertyTorch website — specifically, transferred into the Liberty RWA prize vault on-chain. Simply holding assets or linking a wallet generates zero entries.

Every $0.50 USD worth of qualifying dust actually deposited earns 1 dust entry, up to a maximum of 5 dust entries per linked wallet per funded drawing cycle.

Multiple contributions from the same wallet accumulate toward the five-entry cap. Contributions exceeding $2.50 may still be accepted into the vault but cannot generate more than five entries for that wallet during that cycle.

Maximum Entries Per Account

26 entries maximum per primary account per funded drawing cycle:

  • 1 base entry (from holding $LIBERTY)
  • 25 dust entries (5 wallets × 5 entries each)

Primary wallet dust: Whether the primary wallet may also contribute RWA dust for bonus entries is a separate configuration decision and is not yet confirmed.

Drawing Mechanics

The system checks the prize pool every hour. A drawing takes place when the pool contains at least $50 USD in eligible prizes. If the pool is below $50, the drawing is skipped and funds roll over to the following hour.

Each funded drawing selects 10 unique winners by weighted random draw. The winning account and all its linked wallets are excluded from subsequent drawings for a rolling 24-hour period. Wallet holdings and prior dust contributions remain intact.

Prize Distribution

10 winners per funded draw. Each winner receives a percentage of every asset in the vault, paid in kind — nothing is swapped into a single currency.

Vault Funding

The prize pool is funded by two streams:

  • Tax stream: 1.35% of every $LIBERTY buy automatically flows into the vault — on-chain, every trade.
  • Dust stream: RWA dust deposited through the website is held in the vault, increasing the pool.

Wallet Linking

Secondary wallets must be linked through cryptographic ownership verification via the LibertyTorch website. A signed message proves wallet control — no gas, no transaction. Linking alone generates zero entries; only on-chain vault deposits count.

10. FAQ

Is this affiliated with Torch, Robinhood, or the Statue of Liberty?

No. LibertyTorch is a community project. $LIBERTY is not issued by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Torch, Robinhood Markets, Inc., or the National Park Service.

Where can I buy $LIBERTY?

After launch, through the Torch trading interface or any DEX on Robinhood Chain.

What's the anti-snipe protection?

Torch applies an 80% tax on the first 15–30 seconds of trading, then normalizes.

Can the tax rate change?

No. The 3% buy tax is set at launch and fixed forever.

How do I enter the raffle?

Hold $10+ USD worth of $LIBERTY in your primary wallet for one base entry. Link up to five secondary wallets through the website. Contribute RWA dust through the LibertyTorch website — it must be deposited into the vault on-chain. Every $0.50 deposited earns 1 dust entry, up to 5 per linked wallet per funded draw cycle.

Does linking a wallet give me entries?

No. Only verified, completed dust deposits into the vault generate entries. Linking a wallet is required to associate a secondary wallet with your account, but linking alone produces zero entries.

What's the maximum I can earn?

26 entries per primary account per funded drawing cycle: 1 base entry plus up to 25 dust entries (5 wallets × 5 entries each). The primary wallet may also contribute dust — this is a separate configuration decision.

What happens if the prize pool is below $50?

The drawing is skipped. All unspent funds roll over to the following hour. The system checks every hour.

Can I win more than once in a day?

No. Once you win, your account and all its linked wallets are excluded from drawings for 24 hours. After that window, you can qualify again.

What can I send as dust?

Any allow-listed ERC-20 RWA token. USD value is calculated via oracle pricing at the time of deposit. The final supported token list is still being finalized.

What are prizes paid in?

In kind — your share of every asset in the vault, paid directly to your wallet. Nothing is swapped into a single currency.

How are winners chosen?

Weighted random draw. Entry weight = base entry (1) + dust entries from qualifying deposits. Verifiable randomness is used to ensure fairness.

Where do I get ETH for Robinhood Chain?

Robinhood Crypto supports ETH purchases, or bridge from another network.
