Small holdings.
A shared prize vault.

A new chapter for the little pieces in your wallet.
Here’s the proposed journey, from deposit to draw.

  1. 01Your little pieces

    Put them together.

    Send an allow-listed tokenized asset to the shared vault. Every eligible deposit becomes part of the pool.

  2. 02A little balance

    Give them weight.

    An oracle locks the USD entry value at deposit. After a 2% fee, the net value determines your entry weight.

  3. 03A slice of everything

    Share the possibility.

    Draws are proposed every 10 minutes. Winners receive a proportional share of each asset in the vault.

Wallet connection is optional for personal stats in the proposed flow.

The questions worth asking
before the torch is lit.

What is a real-world asset token?+

It’s a token that represents exposure to an asset such as a stock or fund. The precise rights depend on its issuer. This concept uses examples of tokenized stocks and an index fund.

Can I enter the raffle now?+

Not yet. This is an interactive concept preview. The vault has not been connected to a deployed contract, and no deposit address or transaction flow is available.

How would my entry weight be calculated?+

The proposed oracle locks a USD value when an eligible deposit arrives. A 2% protocol fee is deducted, and the net contribution determines entry weight. The calculator shows this arithmetic only; final protocol rounding and draw rules still need to be defined.

What does a winner receive?+

A proportional share of every asset in the prize vault. For example, the draft first-place share is 35% of each asset, rather than a single cash payout. The final payout basis still needs confirmation.

Why does the payout split total 96%?+

The source proposal leaves 4% unassigned. We are showing that split as a draft until its purpose or the percentages are confirmed. The missing 4% has not been assumed to be a protocol fee.

Are $LIBERTY and TORCH raffle deposits?+

They are part of Lady Liberty’s community identity. They are not shown as eligible raffle deposits here. The live allow-list and token addresses will need to be confirmed before launch.
