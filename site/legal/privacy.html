Privacy Policy

Effective: October 22, 2026

🌿 No accounts. No cookies. No tracking. LibertyTorch is a decentralized protocol. The website at libertytorch.online does not collect personal information, operate accounts, or track visitors. Blockchain interactions are pseudonymous by design.

1. Overview

LibertyTorch ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website at libertytorch.online (the "Site") and the LibertyTorch decentralized raffle protocol on Robinhood Chain (the "Protocol"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with the Site.

This Privacy Policy complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), and applicable New York State privacy laws, including the New York SHIELD Act (General Business Law §899-aa).

2. Information We Collect

LibertyTorch collects no personally identifiable information (PII) through the Site. Specifically:

2.1 Blockchain Data

The Protocol operates on Robinhood Chain. Blockchain transactions, wallet addresses, token balances, and smart contract interactions are publicly visible on-chain. We do not collect, link, or process blockchain data in connection with the Site.

2.2 Wallet Connection

When you connect a cryptocurrency wallet to the Site, the Site reads your public wallet address from your connected wallet provider (e.g., Rabby, MetaMask). This address is used solely to interact with the Protocol's smart contracts. We do not store, log, or transmit wallet addresses beyond the duration of your browser session.

2.3 Off-Chain Signatures

The Protocol may request that you sign an EIP-712 message to link secondary wallets to a primary account. This signature is generated locally in your browser wallet and is submitted directly to the Protocol's smart contracts. LibertyTorch does not receive, store, or process the content of these signatures.

2.4 No Analytics, Cookies, or Trackers

The Site uses no cookies, analytics services, tracking pixels, or third-party monitoring tools. We do not collect:

  • IP addresses, device identifiers, or browser fingerprints
  • Browsing history or clickstream data
  • Email addresses, names, or contact information
  • Location data

3. How We Use Information

Because we collect no personal information, we have no information to use for any purpose beyond the technical operation of the Site.

The Site may display live data from the Protocol's smart contracts (e.g., prize pool size, raffle status, token supply). This data is read directly from Robinhood Chain via a public RPC endpoint and is not processed or stored by us.

5. Data Retention

LibertyTorch does not maintain any user databases, logs of visitor activity, or records of blockchain interactions beyond what is publicly visible on-chain. We have no data to retain, delete, or transfer.

If you have submitted a query or request via email to libertytorch@proton.me, we retain that communication only for the purpose of responding and for a period not exceeding 24 months from the date of receipt.

6. California Residents (CCPA Rights)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information. Because LibertyTorch collects no personal information as defined by the CCPA, we have no information to disclose, sell, or delete in response to any such request.

However, if you believe we have inadvertently collected information in connection with the Site, you may contact us at libertytorch@proton.me to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any such information.

LibertyTorch does not sell personal information and has no intention of doing so.

7. New York Residents (SHIELD Act)

The New York Stop Hacks and Improve Electronic Data Security Act (SHIELD Act), General Business Law §899-aa, requires businesses that collect private information of New York residents to implement specific data security safeguards. LibertyTorch collects no private information as defined by the SHIELD Act (which includes name in combination with a data element such as Social Security number, driver's license number, or financial account information). Accordingly, the technical, physical, and administrative safeguards required under the SHIELD Act are not applicable to any data we hold, because we hold none.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site and Protocol are not directed to, and LibertyTorch does not knowingly collect personal information from, anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that a person under 18 has provided personal information through the Site, we will take commercially reasonable steps to delete that information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has interacted with the Site, please contact us at libertytorch@proton.me.

9. Data Security

Because LibertyTorch collects no personal information, there is no personal data stored on our servers to secure. The Protocol's smart contracts and the Site are maintained with reasonable technical and administrative safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data involved — which, in this case, is none.

Blockchain transactions are secured by cryptographic consensus on Robinhood Chain and are outside the scope of this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

LibertyTorch may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
