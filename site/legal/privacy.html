Privacy Policy

How Lady Liberty handles your data

Not financial advice. Nothing on this site constitutes financial, legal, or investment advice. Cryptocurrency assets involve substantial risk of loss. Always consult a qualified professional before making financial decisions.

1. Information We Collect

Lady Liberty is a decentralized protocol operating on the Robinhood Chain. We do not collect, store, or process personal data on our servers. All protocol interactions occur directly on-chain and are publicly visible.

Information collected automatically

When you visit this website, our hosting provider (Vercel) may collect standard server logs, including your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. This data is used solely for operational and security purposes and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Wallet information

To use the protocol, you connect a self-custody cryptocurrency wallet (such as MetaMask or Rabby). Your wallet address, blockchain transactions, and on-chain balances are publicly visible by design and are not collected or controlled by us.

2. How We Use Information

Automatically collected server data is used to:

  • Maintain website availability and performance
  • Detect and prevent unauthorized access and abuse
  • Generate aggregate analytics to understand site usage

3. Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. Our site may use essential session cookies necessary for navigation. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

4. Third-Party Services

This website interacts with third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Robinhood Chain — All protocol operations (deposits, draws, wallet linking) execute on the Robinhood Chain. Review Robinhood's privacy policy for their data practices.
  • Wallet Extensions — MetaMask, Rabby, and similar wallets collect data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control and are not responsible for their data practices.
  • Vercel — Our hosting provider. See Vercel's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Retention

Server logs are retained for a limited period and then automatically deleted. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal information beyond what is publicly recorded on-chain.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data beyond standard server logs, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. For on-chain data associated with your wallet address, you control your private keys and can interact with any block explorer directly.

7. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect our servers and this website. However, no internet transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee the security of data transmitted over the internet to or from your wallet.

8. International Users

This site and the Lady Liberty protocol are accessible globally. If you are accessing from outside the United States, you consent to the transfer and processing of data in the United States or wherever our hosting infrastructure is located.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the site after any change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at the community channels listed on libertytorch.online.
